Traditional RAG vs Federated RAG (FRAG)

Live Dataset Mode

Research Objective

Evaluate whether federated retrieval across distributed hospital knowledge bases improves retrieval quality, response quality, and healthcare recommendations compared with Traditional RAG.

Research outputs only — not medical diagnosis or a substitute for professional care.

RAG vs FRAG Architecture Overview

Traditional RAG Query → one combined knowledge base → Top-K evidence → LLM
FRAG Query → independent hospital knowledge bases → candidates → fusion/reranking → global Top-K evidence → LLM
